How it works
Each ingredient group sits next to the procedure steps that use it, like a Modernist Cuisine card. Scaling is relative to the 100% base ingredient (click any scaling value to make it the base).

Sub-recipe rows link to their own sheet with a coloured connector. Drag a sheet by its title bar, scroll to zoom, and drag the background to pan.

Preview hides the editing controls. Print prints every sheet, one per page.
